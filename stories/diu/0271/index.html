Tuvoko tuviri twakukwaterera.

Two little hands to hold.

Tughuru tuvuri twakutanga.

Two little feet to kick.

Tuntjo tuvi twakenga.

Two little eyes to see.

Matwi maviri ghamadidi ghakuyuva.

Two little ears to hear.

Ntani makaha maviri ghakudodera.

And two loving arms to HUG!

Written by: Carole Bloch
Illustrated by: Richard MacIntosh
Translated by: Peter L Likoro
Language: Rumanyo
Level: Level 1
